# Session – 2026-05-21

## Topics covered

* KPI dashboards.
* KPI definitions and KPI contracts.
* Data visualization for business metrics.
* Using SQL aggregation functions to calculate KPIs.

## What I understood

* A KPI is more than just a query or a number displayed on a dashboard.
* It requires a clear business objective and a well-defined way to measure progress toward that objective.
* KPIs should be designed carefully, considering edge cases and exceptions that could affect the results.
* Aggregation functions are often used to calculate the values shown in KPI dashboards.

## What is still confusing
