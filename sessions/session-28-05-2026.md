## What I understood
- OLTP systems are designed for day-to-day operations and handle transactions such as inserts, updates, and deletes efficiently.
- OLAP systems are focused on analytics, reporting, and large-scale data analysis rather than operational transactions.
- ETL processes are used to extract data from operational systems, transform it into the required format, and load it into a data warehouse for analysis.
- OLTP and OLAP serve different purposes but often work together in data-driven applications.
